# Analysis output format

Use this order:

1. **Conclusion** — concise answer and confidence.
2. **What the source contributes** — research question and genuine novelty.
3. **Claim-to-evidence check** — major claims paired with supporting or contradicting evidence.
4. **Method and results** — important design choices, datasets, baselines, metrics, and outcomes.
5. **Limitations and counterarguments** — what could invalidate or narrow the conclusion.
6. **Relevance to the team** — concrete implications, adoption constraints, and recommended experiments.
7. **Verified vs unresolved** — explicitly list what was checked and what remains unknown.
8. **Sources** — original link plus any primary sources used for cross-checking.

Avoid a generic section-by-section recap. Prefer evidence-backed decisions and actionable next steps.
