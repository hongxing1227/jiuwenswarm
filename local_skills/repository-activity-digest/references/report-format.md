# Repository digest format

Use this order:

1. **Window and status** — repository, exact UTC start/end, mode, counts, and whether catch-up was used.
2. **Executive assessment** — two to five sentences describing the most important change and why it matters.
3. **New issues** — group by theme; include number, title, labels, author, state, and link.
4. **New pull requests** — group by area; include number, title, draft/state, labels, author, and link.
5. **Risks and blockers** — identify release, security, compatibility, CI, review, or ownership risks. Cite evidence.
6. **Recommended actions** — give prioritized, assignable next steps.
7. **Uncertainty** — list anything that requires patch, CI, maintainer, or reproduction evidence.

Do not repeat every field when there are many items. Use compact grouped bullets and preserve links.
